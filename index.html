A Message to a Stranger

Somewhere in the world, one person is trying to reach another.

This is a human-to-human experiment. A message will travel through a public webpage, hoping to reach someone who has never met its sender.

The sender is known only as Stranger No. 1.

This project does not access anyone's private conversations. It does not guarantee that this page will appear in every AI conversation. It simply invites people to discover the experiment and take part voluntarily.

The personal message will appear here soon.

One person. One stranger. One message.
